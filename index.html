සත්කාර Heartfelt Hospitality

Rooted in Sri Lankan Warmth • Minutes from World Heritage Wonders

In Sinhala culture, "Sathkara" (සත්කාර) represents the noble tradition of hospitable care, attentive reception, and welcoming strangers like family. Nestled along Freeman Mawatha, our guest house was built to provide travelers with an immaculate, restful base while discovering the ancient capital of Anuradhapura.

Whether you are on a spiritual pilgrimage to the sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi tree or exploring the massive brick stupas of Ruwanwelisaya and Jetavanaramaya, return to quiet air-conditioned bedrooms, hot water geyser showers, and warm evening garden dining.
