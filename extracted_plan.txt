RESTAURANT & ACCOMMODATION WEBSITE | DEVELOPMENT PLAN
1. Project overview
The proposed platform combines a public restaurant and accommodation website with a customer account
area and a room reservation workflow. Visitors can explore room categories and prices, view the restaurant
menu, request a safari through the restaurant, contact the massage service, and make room or table
enquiries.
The room booking form calculates the accommodation estimate automatically. Booking requests are stored
in a database and WhatsApp is used as the primary communication and notification channel. Hotel staff still
verify availability and confirm pending requests.
Important: WhatsApp is the notification and communication layer. The booking database
remains the system of record; WhatsApp messages alone are not a reliable booking
database.

4. Room booking system and automatic
pricing
The booking journey should be short, mobile-friendly and divided into clear steps. The customer must see
an updated accommodation estimate whenever the selected dates, room categories or quantities change.
Step Customer action System behaviour
1 Choose check-in and check-out dates Validate dates; checkout must be after check-in; calculate nights
2 Select room category and quantity Show room inventory and date-based availability
3 Enter adults and children Validate the selected room capacities and guest counts
4 Enter contact details Collect full name, phone, email and special requests
5 Select optional services Record safari, massage and table requests separately
6 Review price summary Calculate accommodation total on the server and display it
7 Submit request Create booking reference and save status as Pending
8 WhatsApp notification Notify the restaurant or open a prefilled WhatsApp flow
9 Hotel verification Staff verify availability and communicate confirmation
Price calculation
Accommodation total = sum of (room quantity × nightly rate × number of nights)
Example selection Calculation Total
2 Dual Rooms × 3 nights 2 × Rs. 4,000 × 3 Rs. 24,000
1 Triple + 1 Dual × 2 nights (Rs. 6,000 + Rs. 4,000) × 2 Rs. 20,000
Booking form fields
Field Requirement

Guests Adults and children
Rooms Room type and quantity; only available inventory can be requested
Customer details Full name, mobile number and email address
Additional services Optional safari, massage and table reservation requests
Special requests Free-text notes
The browser may show an instant estimate, but the server must recalculate and validate

5. Availability, booking statuses and
WhatsApp notifications
Room inventory must be tracked by individual physical room or by a transaction-safe category inventory
model. Date overlap checks must prevent two confirmed reservations from consuming the same room for
overlapping nights.
Availability rules
● Maintain the total inventory: 1 Single, 2 Dual, 3 Triple and 2 Family rooms.
● Check the full stay period, not only the check-in date.
● Exclude cancelled reservations from occupied inventory.
● Recheck availability at request submission and again when the hotel confirms.
● Use a temporary hold or controlled pending-request process to prevent conflicting requests.
● Handle simultaneous booking submissions safely in the database.
Booking status model
Status Meaning
Pending Customer submitted a request; hotel has not confirmed it
Confirmed Hotel verified availability and accepted the reservation
Completed Customer stay is finished
Cancelled Booking was cancelled
Rejected Hotel could not accept the request
WhatsApp notification workflow
Stage Action
Customer submits Website validates and saves the booking
Backend event A booking reference is generated and notification is queued
Restaurant notification WhatsApp message is sent to +94 77 863 8229
Hotel follow-up Restaurant contacts supplier if needed, checks room availability and contacts customer
Status update Customer dashboard reflects Pending, Confirmed, Rejected or Cancelled
WhatsApp implementation choices
Click-to-chat: the website creates a prefilled booking message and the customer taps Send. This is simple
but not automatic delivery; it depends on the customer sending the message.
WhatsApp Business Platform/API: the backend can send notifications automatically after a booking is
saved. This requires a WhatsApp Business setup, API credentials, approved templates where applicable and
delivery/error handling.
Recommendation: use the WhatsApp Business Platform if the requirement is automatic
restaurant notifications without asking the customer to manually send a message.

6. Cancellation policy and customer
dashboard
Customers can cancel bookings themselves, subject to the business cancellation policy. The customer
confirmed automatic cancellation as the desired workflow, but the cancellation deadline and any fee have
not yet been supplied.
Cancellation workflow
● Customer opens My Bookings or a secure guest booking link.
● Customer selects Cancel Booking and reviews a confirmation prompt.
● Backend checks whether the booking is eligible for cancellation.
● If eligible, the booking status changes to Cancelled and the event is recorded.
● Availability is recalculated and the customer receives an acknowledgement.
● Cancelled bookings cannot be reactivated by the customer.
Business decision still required: define the cancellation cut-off (for example 24 or 48 hours
before check-in) and whether cancellation fees apply. Do not publish a free-cancellation
promise until approved.

Access methods
Registered customer: create an account, verify it, log in and manage bookings in the dashboard. Bookings
are associated with the authenticated profile.
Guest customer: book without registration, receive a unique booking reference and access booking details
through a secure, unguessable management link or verification flow.
If a guest later registers, provide a secure process to link eligible previous bookings to the new account.

Security and reliability requirements
● Customers may read and manage only their own bookings and profile.
● Guest management links must use secure, unguessable tokens and avoid exposing sequential booking
IDs.
● Enforce database permissions with Row Level Security.
● Never expose WhatsApp API credentials or service-role keys in frontend code.
● Recalculate prices and validate room availability on the server.
● Use transaction-safe booking allocation to avoid overbooking.
● Record cancellation and status changes for traceability.
● Log notification success and failure; provide a fallback contact process.

11. Key user journeys and acceptance criteria
Room booking journey
Visitor opens Rooms → selects dates → chooses room type and quantity → enters guest and contact details
→ sees automatic estimate → submits request → receives reference → restaurant receives notification →
hotel verifies and confirms → customer tracks status.
Safari journey
Customer opens Experiences → selects Safari Request → enters date and participants → submits →
restaurant receives request → restaurant contacts supplier → restaurant returns availability and price to
customer.
Massage journey
Customer opens Massage → taps Call or WhatsApp, or submits a session request → customer and massage
provider coordinate directly.
Acceptance criteria
● The booking calculator correctly multiplies room rate, quantity and number of nights.
● Checkout date must be later than check-in date.
● Room quantities cannot exceed available inventory for the entire stay.
● A booking request is stored with a unique reference and Pending status.
● A WhatsApp notification is generated according to the selected integration method.
● A customer can view only their own account bookings.
● A guest can access their booking only through a secure lookup or tokenized link.
● Eligible cancellation updates the booking status and releases inventory.
● Menu items with unknown prices are not assigned invented prices.
● The landline appears only in the footer.
● All mobile Call and WhatsApp actions use +94 77 863 8229.
